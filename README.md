## Projects

**SceneFun3D Benchmark Challenge** | *Jul – Aug 2026*\
For the OpenSUN3D Workshop challenge at ECCV 2026, I reproduced and extended UniFunc3D for task-driven affordance grounding. The system uses Qwen3-VL and SAM3 to identify the functional part across views and lift the masks into 3D. My test submission scored **4.17 AP50 / 12.56 AP25**.\
[Project](https://junhyeok101.github.io/#projects) · [Leaderboard](https://eval.ai/web/challenges/challenge-page/2466/leaderboard/6121)

**Less is More in 3D** | *Mar – Jun 2026*\
Our B3HIND team developed this AI Capstone Design project in Yonsei University's Department of Computer Science in collaboration with CIP Lab. We combined Sobel-based frame selection and adaptive token merging to make LiteVGGT-based 3D reconstruction more efficient.\
[Project](https://junhyeok101.github.io/#less-is-more-in-3d) · [Code](https://github.com/B3HINDlitevggt/litevggt-adaptive-token-merging) · [Poster (PDF)](https://junhyeok101.github.io/data/b3hind-poster.pdf)
